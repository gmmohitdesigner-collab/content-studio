# Design DNA: Mohit (UX Designer)

## 1. Design Taste
- **Aesthetic:** Minimal, intentional, calm.
- **Leans toward:** Clean layouts, strong spacing, reduced visual noise, premium feel.
- **Values:** Clarity over decoration.
- **Avoids:** Overly flashy UI, trend-heavy visuals without purpose, dense sections.

## 2. Core Philosophy
- **Mantra:** "Design is decision guidance, not decoration."
- **Key Principles:** Structure drives experience, clarity reduces friction, visual hierarchy controls attention.
- **Thinking:** User journey, cognitive load, decision-making.

## 3. Content Structure
- **Focus:** Insight-driven, psychological, structural breakdowns, observational thinking.
- **Avoids:** Generic tips, listicles, surface-level advice.
- **Flow:** Hook → Insight → Explanation → Reinforcement → Close (5–7 slides).

## 4. Visual Standards
- **Priority:** Speed of creation, consistency.
- **Style:** Clean typography, one focal point per slide, controlled emphasis.

## 5. Creative Goals & Audience
- **Target:** Founders, startup teams, product builders, hiring managers.
- **Goal:** Attract first client, build credibility as a conversion-focused UX designer.
- **Positioning:** "A UX designer who understands how users think and how pages convert."

## 6. Tone & Voice
- **Tone:** Clear, sharp, human (not AI-sounding), slightly observational/contrarian.
- **Avoids:** Buzzwords, over-polished GPT tone.

## 7. System Constraints
- Content must be quick to produce.
- System should be reusable and templated.
- Flexibility for iteration without increasing effort.
